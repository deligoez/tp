## Dummy nodes: not added

The closure judged is B3's with its gate made implicit, 55 epics, the size the note names as the
90th percentile. Unselected, the long edges from A2 to B3 cross the B1 and B2 bands as plain curves
and pass behind labels, which keep their halo and stay legible; following one chain by eye there is
hard. Selecting an epic highlights its edges and dims everything else, and the chain then reads at
once. Since selection is how a closure is read, the plain curves stand and dummy nodes were not
added.

## Review

Two roles, one round each (semantic correctness; AX economy and contract), no critical or high
finding. The medium findings were fixed with a test seen red first: two milestones that depend on
each other across tracks gave the gate-closure view an edge pointing backwards (now one band naming
both); the dependency view drew the previous layout when re-centring or changing milestone; the
"stale" review figure counted slices a re-review alone does not settle (now only stale slices that
would otherwise be done and are under their cap); several page tests passed with the behaviour
broken (the decisions moved into `page/pure.js` and are tested under node). The band-cycle and depth
probes were re-run by hand after the fixes.

Left for later, seen in the after screenshots: re-centring the neighbourhood view keeps the zoom, so
at depth 2 the outermost nodes can sit outside the box until Fit is pressed.

## Mutation run

`./scripts/mutation.sh` over the engine files changed since v1.12.0: 98 mutants, 86 killed, 12
escaped. Two escapes named real gaps and gained a test each, both seen red against the mutant: a
chain of single-predecessor steps inside one band (`preds == 0` negated left nodes out of the
longest-path walk), and a layer ordered by its neighbours (the sweep direction). The rest are
ordering quality, not correctness: the barycenter key's arithmetic and the sweep parity change which
of two legal orders a layer takes, and the tests pin legal, stable layouts rather than a crossing
count; one guards a branch no input reaches (`width == 0` against `-1`, `comp` initialised to `-2`),
one is the bands-reporting threshold on a single unnamed span, and one sorts weeks with `<=`, which
the same week never meets twice.
