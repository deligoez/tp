# tp v1.6.0 — what to start next, on a large plan

> Decision track for F2; fix track for the rest. The findings are `spec/backlog/epics-progress-field.md`
> (the v1.5.0 pilot, in shape only). F5 is declined there.

## 1. Decision: a milestone names what it opens after (F2)

A track forces sequential opening, but some milestones of a track are siblings, and one milestone may
open after an earlier one in parallel with its neighbour. The graph gains `opens_after`, a map from a
milestone to the milestones it opens after:

```json
"opens_after": { "G": ["E2"], "L3": [] }
```

- A milestone absent from the map opens after the previous milestone of its track, as today; the
  first milestone of a track opens after none.
- A milestone is open when every milestone it opens after is open and has a green gate. `[]` means
  always open, so a family of sibling milestones stays one track with `[]` on each.
- A named milestone may be in another track. `tp epic lint` errors on an unknown milestone and on a
  cycle; `later-milestone-dependency` keeps reading track order. A warning, `partial-parallel`, fires
  once per pair when a milestone does not open after an earlier milestone of its track yet some of
  its epics depend on that milestone's epics, naming them: the block still makes them wait. It is
  info instead when the milestone's own `opens_after` entry chose that parallelism.
- `tp epic status` reports, per milestone, its effective `opens_after` (defaults applied) and
  `waiting_on`, the milestones of that list whose gate is not green or that are not open.

Alternatives: tracks that branch from a milestone (a second graph inside the first, and the sibling
case still needs one track per sibling); prose inside a threshold (what the field does today, and tp
cannot read it). Non-goal: a condition on opening other than gates.

## 2. Fixes

| # | behaviour |
|---|---|
| F1 | each `tp epic ready` item carries `downstream: {longest_chain, dependents, dependents_open, dependents_by_milestone}`: the most not-done epics on one path of epics depending on it, transitively, itself excluded; the count of not-done epics depending on it, transitively; the part of those in an open milestone; and those counts per milestone (ready items only, not the export). Ready items are ordered by `longest_chain`, then `dependents`, then graph order. `tp epic status` gains a top-level `critical_path`: the longest chain of not-done epics in the whole graph |
| F3 | a condition is listed under `operator` only when it is `actionable`: not done, its dependencies done, its milestone open, and at least one of: it is a member (explicit or effective, `GateMembers`) of the gate of an open milestone; nothing depends on it; or at least one epic depending on it can move (is not done, and is in an open milestone or has a bound file) and has only conditions among its undone direct dependencies (an epic every dependent of which is done, and that holds no open gate, gates nothing and is not actionable); the rest are listed under `--all` with `actionable: false` and `not_actionable`, every reason that holds in the order `milestone` (its milestone closed), `dependencies` (an undone dependency of its own), `dependents` (none of the three holds), and a done condition is listed in neither. `tp epic status` `blocked[]` and `tp epic export` `epics[]` carry the same `not_actionable` on conditions; a condition never carries `waiting_on` |
| F4 | `no-dependencies` does not fire on a condition |
| F6 | a slice without `key.json` reports the key derived from its recorded rounds; `key: null` only when it has none |
| F7 | `tp epic status --open` reports open milestones only |
| F8 | `--epic <id>` on `tp resume`, `tp next`, `tp ready` resolves the epic's bound file: the only one, or the only one not at `release`; otherwise exit 4 naming them |
| F9 | `tp epic export` `epics[]` items repeat `milestone` and `condition` and carry F1's `downstream`, F3's `actionable` (conditions), `held_by` (the undone conditions among an epic's direct dependencies) and `blocked_by` (its undone direct dependencies) |
| B1 | `tp epic set gates.<m>.threshold=<text>`, `gates.<m>.deferrable=<ids>` and `gates.<m>.members=<ids>` edit one gate and carry its `met` |
| B2 | `share_done` and `throughput` leave conditions out; counts report `conditions_done` apart |
| B3 | the roll-up gains `estimate_total`, over every task of an epic's bound files |

The export keeps `version: 1`: every change adds a field, and `share_done`'s meaning narrows (B2),
which the release notes name.
