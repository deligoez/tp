# tp 1.14.0 — a rewritten spec starts a new epoch

1.14.0 builds `spec/backlog/reconcile.md`, a decision, in one clone with one commit series per unit.
Every reproduction below ran in a fresh `mktemp` git repository with a binary built from the clone:
the "before" binary at `b85027fb`, the "after" one at the head of the series. The fixture is
synthetic: a two-section spec, `tp init`, one task added and committed.

## Decision 1 — the epoch

**Before.** Round 1 emitted and recorded with one `high` row whose finding holds the marker
`MARKER-OLD-TEXT`; the spec then replaced by a one-section document sharing no sentence with it;
round 2 emitted. Round 2 emits four prompts, and the three role prompts (implementer, tester,
architect) carry the marker under "UNRESOLVED findings from previous rounds — DO NOT re-report"; the
regression prompt, which lists fixed rows only, does not. Nothing in the CLI could stop it.

**After.** The same sequence with `tp review spec.md --rewritten "spec replaced wholesale on
purpose"` between the rewrite and the emission: the call exits 0 with
`{"spec":"spec.md","epoch":{"after_review_round":1,"after_audit_round":0,"evidence":"…","at":"…"}}`,
round 2's role prompts carry the marker in **none of the three**, `review_loop.previous_findings` is
0, and `tp review spec.md --status` reports the one epoch under `epochs`. Without the flag the after
binary still carries it in all three, so the cut is the epoch's and nothing else's.

The four lists the note names are each cut at the newest epoch, and each has a test that was seen
red on the readers before the cut and green after it:

| List | Reader | Test |
|---|---|---|
| review carry list | `loadReviewRoundState` | `TestEpoch_TheReviewCarryListStartsAtTheEpoch` |
| regression pass, auto-appended and standalone | `collectFixedFindings` | `TestEpoch_TheRegressionPassListsNoFixedRowFromBeforeTheEpoch`, `TestEpoch_StandaloneRegressionListsNoFixedRowFromBeforeTheEpoch` |
| `tp run`'s regression unit | `engine.RegressionDue` | `TestRegressionDue_CountsNoFixedRowFromBeforeTheEpoch` |
| audit Prior Round block | `loadAuditPriorRound` | `TestEpoch_TheAuditPriorRoundSectionStartsAtTheEpoch` |

| `scripts/audit-round-prep.py` | `epoch_cut` | `scripts/audit-round-prep-test.sh`, "an epoch after the previous round exits 2" |

`RegressionDue` is not in the note's list. It is cut because the oracle and the emission share one
predicate since 1.12.0: cutting only the emission would let `tp run` spawn a regression unit for a
prompt that guards nothing the epoch left.

**What does not move.** `TestEpoch_ConvergenceAndTheCapStillCountEveryRound` (two clean rounds, a
rewrite, an epoch: `consecutive_clean` stays 2, `stale` stays true, `rounds_remaining` stays 1; one
more clean round over the new text converges with 3) and `TestEpoch_TheCapStillRefusesTheNextEmission`
(three unclean rounds, an epoch, and the next emission still exits 4) were each seen red under a
deliberate sabotage that read the rounds since the epoch instead of all of them, in `--status` and in
the emission's budget check respectively, then the sabotage was reverted.

## Decision 2 — the blocker names its exit

**Before.** Two converged rounds, round 3 emitted over that text, then the spec edited; `tp resume`
reports `spec-stale` with data `{"spec":"spec.md"}` and the message "run a review round over the new
text: tp review spec.md, then tp review spec.md --record <file>". Running the first command exits 3:
round 3 is in flight and its snapshot differs from the spec.

**After.** The same blocker's data is

```
{"spec":"spec.md","in_flight_round":3,
 "commands":["tp review spec.md --record <file>","tp review spec.md","tp review spec.md --record <file>"],
 "operator":{"decision":"discard-emission","commands":["tp review spec.md --force","tp review spec.md --record <file>"]}}
```

and the message says the same: record round 3 first, then a round over the new text, with the
discard named as the operator's decision. With no round in flight `commands` is the emission and the
record; with one in flight over the current text it is the record alone, and there is no `operator`.

**At the review cap (review round 1).** Three rounds recorded at the default cap of 3, the spec
edited, `tp resume`: the first form gave `commands: ["tp review spec.md", "tp review spec.md --record
<file>"]`, and `tp review spec.md` exits 4. Now `commands` is `[]` and the emission and record sit
under `operator: {decision: "raise-review-cap", …}`, the decision raising `review_max_rounds` already
maps to in `engine.EscalationDecisions`; when recording a round in flight over the old text reaches
the cap, `commands` is that record alone. Tests: `TestSpecStaleBlocker_AtTheReviewCapNamesTheRaiseAsTheOperators`,
`TestSpecStaleBlocker_AtTheCapAfterTheRecordNamesTheRecordAlone`, both seen red first.

## The refusals

`tp review <spec> --rewritten` writes `state.json` only and is placed on the task-file side
(`commandSides`). It exits 2 with blank evidence, with no recorded round in either phase (and then
creates no state directory), and when the epoch would cut nothing in either phase; it exits 3, the
emission guard's code, with a round of either phase in flight. The refusal is decided before the
state lock is taken, since taking it would create the directory, and again under it. `--force`,
`--no-state`, `--docs-path`, `--test-path`, `--round`, `--findings`, `--affected-files`,
`--final-round`, `--diff-from`, `--spec-inline`, `--perspective` and `--role` are refused with exit 2
naming `--rewritten`, and `--merge`, `--resolve`, `--resolve-all`, `--verify`, `--report`, `--record`
and `--status` as mode conflicts; `--docs-path` and `--test-path` passed silently
until review round 1 (`TestReviewRewritten_RefusesTheFlagsOfOtherModes`, seen red on both).

## Where the epoch goes — changed after review round 1

The reviewers read a first form that placed the epoch after each phase's last recorded round and
refused it when either phase's last round hash equalled the spec. That form stranded the other phase
and had a dead end: a round over the new text in flight, `--rewritten` exits 3 "record first", and
after the record the hash matches, so `--rewritten` exits 2 "nothing rewritten". The position was
then redefined (`engine.EpochPosition`): per phase, the number of the last recorded round that read
an older text — a `spec_hash` other than the spec's, or no `snapshot` hash at all (unmarked or
`no-emission`), since such a round's reading cannot be established — and never below the newest
epoch's position. The call is refused with exit 2 only when, in both phases, that equals the newest
epoch's position.

Both dead ends, before (`0d3697f6`, the first form) and after, in fresh `mktemp` repositories:

- *Round in flight over the new text.* Before: `--rewritten` exits 3, the round is recorded, then
  `--rewritten` exits 2 "nothing was rewritten". After: exit 3, record, exit 0 with
  `after_review_round: 1`, keeping round 2; a second `--rewritten` exits 2 "nothing to cut".
  `TestReviewRewritten_RecordingTheRoundInFlightOpensTheEpoch`, `TestReviewRewritten_TwiceInARowIsRefused`.
- *Stranded audit.* Two review rounds and one audit round over H1, the spec rewritten to H2, one
  review round over H2. Before: exit 2 "nothing was rewritten", the audit round over H1 uncut. After:
  exit 0 with `after_review_round: 2`, `after_audit_round: 1`. The test records two rounds of each
  phase and expects 2 and 2: `TestReviewRewritten_ARoundOverTheNewTextStrandsNoAuditRound`.

The position's own cases are pinned in the engine: per phase
(`TestEpochPosition_IsTheLastRoundOverAnOlderTextPerPhase`), unmarked and `no-emission` rounds mixed
with snapshot rounds (`TestEpochPosition_ARoundWithNoSnapshotHashReadAnOlderText`), a spec reverted to
an earlier text, whose first rounds over that text are cut with the rest
(`TestEpochPosition_ARevertedSpecCutsTheEarlyRoundsToo`), and the floor
(`TestEpochPosition_NeverMovesBackwards`).

## Calls the note left open

- **One position per phase.** The note writes `{after_round, evidence, at}`. Review and audit number
  their rounds independently and both phases' readers need a cut, so the epoch stores
  `after_review_round` and `after_audit_round`, each that phase's `EpochPosition`.
- **No recorded round** is refused (exit 2): there is nothing for an epoch to separate.
- **`at`** is an RFC 3339 string like `recorded_at`, not a typed time.
- **`epochs` is omitted while empty**, in `state.json` and on `--status`, so no state file recorded
  before 1.14.0 changes bytes on its next save, and it survives `--compact`.
- **Concurrency.** An emission takes no state lock, so `--rewritten` run concurrently with one is
  unsupported; REFERENCE says so.
- **`scripts/audit-round-prep.py`** takes an epoch position only as a non-negative integer, so a JSON
  bool is no position (`a bool epoch position cuts nothing` in its shell test).

## Downgrade

A binary older than 1.14.0 does not know `epochs` and drops it on its next save of `state.json`,
which silently ends every epoch.

## Left alone

The BUGS.md entry "An unresolved high review finding is converged away by empty rounds" is the
operator's decision and is untouched: the epoch reads convergence exactly as before. Epic review's
carry is out of scope, per the note.

## Review and mutation

Two roles, one round each, no critical or high finding. Both medium findings were dead ends a hint
led into: recording a round over the new text made the epoch refuse as "nothing rewritten", and the
`spec-stale` blocker named an emission the spent budget refuses. The epoch's position was redefined
as above and the blocker now hands the emission to the operator; the two sequences were then re-run
by hand against the built binary, independently of the fixing unit's scripts.

`./scripts/mutation.sh` over the engine files changed since v1.13.0: 156 mutants, 140 killed, 11
escaped, 5 not covered. Three escapes named real gaps and gained a test each, seen red against its
mutant: a history whose only old-text round is round 1 (the walk back stopped before it), a record
that leaves one round under the cap, and a cap of one round. The other escapes are in the state
file's write path, which this release touched only by adding a field: the directory modes and the
two error branches of the link-or-rename save, unchanged since earlier releases.

`go fix ./...` before the release rewrote `EpochPosition`'s backward index loop as a range over
`slices.Backward`; that copies the round struct per iteration and fails the gate's `gocritic`
`rangeValCopy`, so the rewrite was reverted and the index loop kept.
