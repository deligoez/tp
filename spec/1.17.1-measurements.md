# tp 1.17.1 — measurements: the dashboard after the operator's review

What 1.17.1 changed in the dashboard and what it measured doing so. Every figure comes from the
synthetic graph of `spec/1.17.0-measurements.md` (819 epics, 3,141 edges, 20 milestones over five
tracks), served by a freshly built binary on a loopback port; none comes from a requesting project.

## Who decided

- **The operator** reviewed the shipped 1.17.0 card and asked for the six items below: trackpad and
  wheel input, cross edges on by default, a full-screen layout, an Auto theme, structured info
  notes, and readability at scale.
- **tp-smart** gave a second opinion on each item before it was built; the exact rules below (no
  trackpad guess, the layer order, what the address keeps) are its and the operator's together.
- **The readability ideas** — levels of detail by on-screen label size, ids wrapped inside a fixed
  box, region tints far out, hues nudged to a contrast floor — came from a peer project's experience
  and are used here as conclusions. Nothing of that project's code, names or data is in tp.
- **A fresh UI/UX audit** of 1.17.0 ran beside the work; its measured items were folded in (below),
  and where it disagreed with the operator's brief, the brief won.
- **A review round** followed: the operator decided two more items (the drawer as an overlay, and
  "Aydınlık" for the Turkish light theme) and a reviewer's findings were fixed (§ Review round).

## 1. Input, with no trackpad-or-mouse guess

`tpPure.wheelAction(ev, fullscreen)` decides every wheel event: Ctrl or ⌘ held → `zoom` (Chrome's and
Firefox's pinch arrives this way), else `pan` in full screen and `page` outside it. Safari's pinch
arrives as `gesturestart`/`gesturechange`/`gestureend`, heard and cancelled only on the canvas box,
and zooms by `tpPure.pinchZoom(prev, scale)`, the log of the scale's change; while a gesture runs, a
Ctrl-wheel is ignored so a pinch never zooms twice. The wheel listener sits on the canvas box in the
capture phase with `passive: false`; deck.gl's own canvas was measured not to block wheel events.
`tpPure.wheelDelta` reads `deltaMode` as pixels, lines of 16 or pages of the canvas's height.

The zoom step is `-dy / (100 ln 2)` as a power of two, limited to ±0.5 per event. The audit measured
Chrome's pinch gain at 0.69 (a 2× finger spread gave 1.62×); this constant makes it track one to one,
and a mouse notch of 100 zooms 1.41×. The step is not smoothed. The canvas box carries
`overscroll-behavior-x: none`, so a sideways swipe never becomes the browser's back.

Tests: `TestPure_TheWheelZoomsWithAModifierPansInFullScreenAndElseScrollsThePage`,
`TestPure_AWheelsDeltaIsReadInPixels`, `TestPure_AZoomStepIsExponentialAndClampedPerEvent`,
`TestPage_TheCanvasTakesTheWheelAndSafarisPinchBeforeDeckGL`.

## 2. Cross edges on by default

`tpPure.crossShown` reads one flag, `showCross`, in every mode, true until K hides it; `msCross` is
gone. The address carries `cross=0` only when cross edges are hidden; an old link's `cross=1` reads
as the default. `spec/backlog/dashboard-graph.md` is amended: the operator changed decision 2's default
after reviewing the card.

The edge layers run, bottom to top: bands, plain cross edges, any-of edges, long tree edges, short
tree edges, the critical path, nodes, labels (`tpPure.edgeLayers`). A long edge spans three layers or
eight columns (`tpPure.edgeLong`). The styles are the audit's, rendered and compared on both themes
(its screenshots 35 and 37): cross edges far out at opacity 24/255 on light and 28 on dark, width 0.6,
nearer 40/45 at 0.75; tree edges in the ink at 170/150, width 1.4; long edges fainter than their
class. With an epic selected, its chain draws at width 2.5 at the chain's opacity (full within two
steps, the existing fade beyond unless "Show the whole chain" is on), its cross edges whatever the
toggle, and every other edge at 12/255.

Drawn counts, read from the card in every full-screen shot (`ui-1171/drawn.tsv`): Plan 819 nodes and
all 3,141 edges, with an epic selected too; Gate closure A3 101 and 333; Neighbourhood of
`api-gw-replay` at depth 2, 60 and 112. With cross edges on, every layout edge is drawn.

## 4. Theme

The header's control is Auto · Light · Dark (Otomatik · Aydınlık · Koyu), each with an icon (monitor,
sun, moon) beside its word and `aria-pressed`. Auto is the default and stores nothing; choosing it
removes the stored value, and Light or Dark is stored. On Auto a change of the system's setting
restyles the page and the graph live: `prefers-color-scheme` fires `tp-theme`, and the graph bumps
its theme version, which every layer's update triggers hold. Checked in agent-browser by switching
the emulated media from dark to light: the theme version went 0 → 1 and the canvas colour from
`[16, 22, 28]` to `[238, 241, 245]` without a reload.

In Turkish the light theme is "Aydınlık": the operator changed the glossary's "Açık" in the review
round, since it also names an open milestone (`TestPage_TheTurkishLightThemeIsAydinlik`; the 1.17.0
glossary and the review table say so).

## 5. Info notes

All 26 notes, and the new keys note, are arrays of bullets in `en.json` and `tr.json`, as many in
Turkish as in English, with no syntax of their own: their only markup is `<kbd>`, `<code>` and `<b>`.
`t.list(key)` gives them as markup, values escaped. A note opens as a heading (the title of its card,
figure or drawer) and a list, at most `min(420px, 100vw − 32px)` wide, `min(70vh, 560px)` tall and
scrolling, named by its heading through `aria-labelledby`. Focus moves to its one × button; Esc or ×
closes it and gives focus back to the (i), and Esc stops there, so a drawer beside it stays open.

The budget is the audit's: a lead of at most 25 words, at most 6 bullets of at most 20 words, a
source line of at most 25 (`TestPage_EveryInfoIsAShortBulletList`). The five longest (graph,
waiting, band, cfd, loops) were rewritten first, then the rest. The parity test now also requires the
same number of bullets in both languages.

## 6. Readability at scale

- **Levels of detail** by the label's size on screen (`tpPure.detailLevel`): below 9 px no text and no
  box borders but the selection's cone, a search's matches and held marks, each fill leaning 45 %
  toward its band's area colour so the far view reads as regions; below 12 px the id's first line;
  from 12 px the whole id. The audit raised the old 7 px floor to 9. The tooltip names the id and
  title at every level. The neighbourhood opens at zoom 0.13, where an id is 12 px.
- **Wrapped ids** (`tpPure.wrapId`): a box is 150 units wide and 28 tall and holds 20 characters a
  line; a longer id breaks after the last `.`, `-` or `:` in the line's second half, else at the
  line's end, and a second line too long ends in `…`. The box never widens.
- **Small graphs** (a gate closure, a neighbourhood, or under 200 nodes) fit no further out than a
  9-pixel label (`tpPure.fitFloor`), aligned to the top.
- **Band labels** are pills on the canvas's colour inside their band's top-left corner, still placed
  so no two overlap (`tpPure.placeLabels`).
- **Colours** (`tpPure.graphPalette`): each status hue and edge colour is moved toward white on the
  dark canvas, toward black on the light one, a twentieth at a time, until it reads at 3:1 on the
  canvas; each node fill eases toward the card until the ink reads on it at 4.5:1.
  `TestPage_EveryColourPairReadsAgainstItsBackground` parses `style.css`'s tokens for both themes, runs
  them through the same palette under node, and fails if any text on a node fill is under 4.5:1 or any
  border, edge or the (i) ring is under 3:1. The audit's low pairs are fixed and covered: tab counts
  (`--faint` 2.9:1 → `--muted`), legend items toggled off (opacity .4 → `--muted` with a line
  through), the search's placeholder (`#757575` → `--muted`), and the (i) ring (~1.6:1 → `--muted`).

## Review round

**The operator's decisions.**

- **A. The drawer is an overlay.** It slides in from the right over the page, a backdrop dims the rest,
  and the page keeps its width; a click on the backdrop or Esc closes it, focus moves in and comes
  back as before, and a phone keeps its bottom sheet. This reverses the 1.13.0 rule of
  `spec/backlog/dashboard-ux.md` §2, amended there (`TestPage_TheDrawerIsAnOverlayOverADimmedPage`).
  The KPI row no longer reflows when the drawer opens.
- **B. "Aydınlık"** for the Turkish light theme (§4).

**The overflow check**, run in the browser for every mode, drawer closed and open, English and Turkish,
at 1440 × 900 and 1920 × 1080 (`ui-1171/overflow.tsv`): the pill must not scroll and Fit and Exit
must end left of the drawer. Its first run failed one case of 32: Turkish Neighbourhood beside the
drawer at 1440, 1048 px of content in 974. After the second fix all 32 pass; at 1440 with the drawer
open the widest, Turkish in every mode, ends Exit at 979 px with the drawer at 1000.

Each red was seen before its fix: the six new page tests failed on the first round's code; the dark
blocks' test was seen red by changing one token of the system's dark block alone; the ΔE floor by the
measured 2.7 above.
