# 1.6.0 — measurements

The sidecar of `spec/1.6.0.md`: how the cycle ran. Grounding does not grade this file.

## Tracks

The findings came from the requesting project's pilot of v1.5.0 on its own graph, recorded in shape
only in `spec/backlog/epics-progress-field.md`: a first batch (F1–F9) and a second on the released
v1.5.0 (B1–B3). F5 was declined there. F2 changes which milestones are open, a choice between
alternatives with user-visible consequences, so it took the decision track: §1 of `spec/1.6.0.md` is
its note, with the alternatives and the non-goal. Every other item had a reproduction and took the
fix track: a failing test first, then the fix and the gate.

## Slices and the pilot

The work shipped in six slices, and the requesting project piloted each on its graph before the
next one started; a slice's pilot findings were built at the start of the next. It also wrote
`spec/1.6.0-checks.md`: acceptance checks in generic terms, no names from its graph, each turned
into a synthetic fixture and one test. A check already green before its change was kept as a
regression guard and said so; every other check was seen red first.

- **Slice 0** — F4, B2, B3: conditions out of `no-dependencies`, `share_done` and `throughput`, and
  `estimate_total`. Its pilot found nothing.
- **Slice 1** — F1, the downstream counts, the ranking and the graph's critical path. Its pilot
  found S1-1, `dependents_by_milestone` better read as an ordered list than an object, and S1-2,
  `dependents_open` counting every open track head, which the docs now say, pointing to the
  per-milestone counts.
- **Slice 2** — S1-1, then F2: `opens_after`, its lint and its status fields. Its pilot found S2-1, a
  one-key edit silently re-routing the later milestones of a track, and S2-2, a `partial-parallel`
  warning on parallelism the graph chose outright; and it confirmed that a milestone without a
  `gates` entry had no review unit, so its `opens_after` was reviewed nowhere.
- **Slice 3** — S2-1 (`ancestry_changed`), S2-2 (the `info` severity), a gate unit for every
  milestone, then F3, actionable conditions. Its pilot narrowed the rule to count only a dependent
  that can move, and found S3-1, a refused write's generic hint.
- **Slice 4** — the narrowed F3, S3-1 (one hint per broken rule), B1 and F7. Its pilot found S4-1, an
  unknown id in a gate's list given the edge hint.
- **Slice 5** — S4-1, which showed that `gate-member-outside-milestone` never read `deferrable`; the
  review's mechanized classes, which left out `opens-after-cycle`; then F6, F8 and F9. Its pilot
  asked for `title` in the export's epic items, added last.

## What the docs carry

Every output shape or choice the spec left open was recorded as it was built and documented from
that record, each shape checked against the built binary on a throwaway fixture. The spec's F3 row
was edited twice during the cycle, once to widen the actionable rule to standalone and
condition-only waits and once to narrow it to a dependent that can move.
