# 1.6.0 — measurements

The sidecar of `spec/1.6.0.md`: how the cycle ran. Grounding does not grade this file.

## Tracks

The findings came from the requesting project's pilot of v1.5.0 on its own graph, recorded in shape
only in `spec/backlog/epics-progress-field.md`: a first batch (F1–F9) and a second on the released
v1.5.0 (B1–B3). F5 was declined there. F2 changes which milestones are open, a choice between
alternatives with user-visible consequences, so it took the decision track: §1 of `spec/1.6.0.md` is
its note, with the alternatives and the non-goal. Every other item had a reproduction and took the
fix track: a failing test first, then the fix and the gate.

## Slices and the pilot

The work shipped in five slices, and the requesting project piloted each on its graph before the
next one started. It also wrote `spec/1.6.0-checks.md`: acceptance checks in generic
terms, no names from its graph, each turned into a synthetic fixture and one test. A check already
green before its change was kept as a regression guard and said so; every other check was seen red
first.

- **Slice 0** — F4, B2, B3: conditions out of `no-dependencies`, `share_done` and `throughput`, and
  `estimate_total`.
- **Slice 1** — F1, the downstream counts, the ranking and the graph's critical path. Its pilot
  found S1-1, `dependents_by_milestone` better read as an ordered list than an object, and S1-2,
  `dependents_open` counting every open track head, which the docs now say, pointing to the
  per-milestone counts.
- **Slice 2** — F2, `opens_after`, its lint and its status fields. Its pilot found S2-1, a one-key
  edit silently re-routing the later milestones of a track, answered by `ancestry_changed`, and S2-2,
  a `partial-parallel` warning on parallelism the graph chose outright, answered by the `info`
  severity. The same slice found that a milestone without a `gates` entry had no review unit, so its
  `opens_after` was reviewed nowhere; every milestone now has one.
- **Slice 3** — F3, actionable conditions. The rule was narrowed during the slice to count only a
  dependent that can move. Its pilot found S3-1, a refused write's generic hint, answered by one hint
  per broken rule.
- **Slice 4** — B1, F6, F7, F8, F9. Its pilot found S4-1, an unknown id in a gate's list given the
  edge hint; fixing it showed that `gate-member-outside-milestone` never read `deferrable`, and that
  the review's mechanized classes left out `opens-after-cycle`. The export's epic items gained
  `title` last.

## What the docs carry

Every output shape or choice the spec left open was recorded as it was built and documented from
that record, each shape checked against the built binary on a throwaway fixture. The spec's F3 row
was edited twice during the cycle, once to widen the actionable rule to standalone and
condition-only waits and once to narrow it to a dependent that can move.
