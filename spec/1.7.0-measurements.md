`direct` tag rows in one `apply`; a 308-row round-1 repair plan replayed through `apply` in one

epics; `--grant-round` refused below the cap and granted once at it; `--merge -o -`; batch emit,

regression-round brief, `key` on batch emission, track order, the legacy-slice next action, and the
