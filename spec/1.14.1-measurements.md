# tp 1.14.1 — the dashboard at a real plan's size, second pass: measurements

A fix-track patch to `tp dashboard`. A field run of 1.13.0's page on a large real plan found nine
things; the fixes were decided with the tp-smart session before the build, and this file records,
as conclusions, how each was reproduced on a synthetic graph, what changed and what shows it. Field
numbers stay out: every figure below is from the synthetic graph.

## The synthetic graph

1.13.0's audit generator (216 epics, 9 milestones in 3 tracks), extended until each finding
reproduced: 309 epics (8 of them conditions) in 14 milestones and 4 tracks. The additions:

- a fourth track C1-C5 opening after A1. C2's gate takes every C2 work epic and its epics lean hard
  on C1, so C2's closure is 70 epics over 2 bands and 18 layers; C3-C5 make the milestone chain six
  layers long;
- two trigger-track blockers: T1 and T2 open on a trigger (`opens_after: []`), and one T1 epic and
  one T2 epic are waited on by 12 and 8 epics of open milestones;
- long threshold and purpose texts on A2, B2 and C2;
- a specced epic: an unclaimed A2 epic whose dependencies are done, with three A2 epics waiting on
  it, bound to a spec with `tp init <spec> --epic <id>`.

The generator and its setup script live outside the repository with the agent that ran the cycle,
as 1.13.0's did. Screenshots were taken at 1440x900, light theme, before (the 1.14.0 binary) and
after (this branch).

Each test was seen red for the stated reason before its fix: a missing pure function, the old

The contained first view of a 70-epic closure opens at about 0.4 screen pixels per unit, an overview
whose labels need a zoom to read. That is the decision as given (Fit holds the whole drawing); the
Plan map keeps its readable first view, since it is not a scoped view.

The rule finding 3 states is written into `spec/backlog/dashboard-ux.md`: a number shown beside a
drawing either counts what is drawn or says what else it counts.

## Dummy nodes: still not added

The cheap remedy went first, as decided: opaque label boxes over edges drawn behind the nodes. Then
C2's closure, 70 epics over 2 bands, was looked at again zoomed in twice from the contained view,
unselected and with a C1 epic in its middle selected. No edge is drawn over a node box any more:
every edge passes behind the boxes and marks between its ends, and none runs through text. What is left
is a bundle of near-parallel long edges from C1 into C2, which an unselected eye cannot follow one
by one; dummy nodes would route the same bundle through dummy slots in the same layers and not
separate it. Selecting an epic highlights its edges and dims the rest, and the chain then reads.
So routing through dummy nodes was not added, and the remaining cost is the bundle, read through
selection. Screenshots: `after-closure-C2-zoom.png` and `after-closure-C2-selected-zoom.png`, beside
`before-closure-C2.png`.

## Review

One review of the diff; its findings were fixed with a test seen red first:

- A card's Show all was decided by one measurement, which reads zero heights in a hidden tab, so a
  page opened on Specs showed no control on any card after switching to Epics. `tpPure.cut` now says
  "unknown" for a box with no height, and the cards are measured again when `#msstate` changes size
  and on opening Epics: the three long cards (A2, B2, C2) show the control after the switch.
- Finding 5's fix made an epic past planned lose its blocked mark, while the KPI and Waiting still
  counted it blocked. `markState` now keeps both facts: the stage as the mark, the hold as a ring
  (with a slash when blocked) and in its words. On the synthetic graph 6 of the 9 claimed epics read
  "claimed, blocked".
- Toggling a card, a Waiting section or a section's Show all redrew the page and dropped focus to the
  body; focus now returns to the replacement button through its data key.
- Smaller: the card control's label names its milestone, a section with no milestone says so in its
  Show all, and the section, re-centring and totals decisions moved into pure functions with tests.
  The Lifecycle-over-time colours were left: the map draws claimed, specced and in progress in one
  colour told apart by shape, so matching it would draw three stacked bands of the chart alike.
  Telling them apart there takes new colour tokens, more than a token change.
