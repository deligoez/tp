# v1.27.0 measurements

v1.27.0 rebuilds the dashboard page on `DESIGN.md` (Practical UI, CLAUDE.md rule 14), simplifies its
Epics view, lets `tp dashboard` fall back to a free port when the default one is taken, and fixes three
timing races in the engine's crash and rolling tests. The decisions are in
`spec/backlog/dashboard-redesign.md` and `spec/backlog/dashboard-simplify.md`; this file keeps what the
cycle measured.

## Dashboard redesign

Track: Decision, built on a branch, two reviewer roles (design conformance, behaviour regression), two
rounds. Fixture: a synthetic project (14 epics, 3 task files, owners), shot light/dark x EN/TR x 1440/390.

| Epics view, 1440x900, EN, light | Before | Simplify pass 1 | Simplify pass 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| KPI cards | 9 | 4 | 4 |
| Controls | 72 | 60 | 56 |
| Visible words | 550 | 298 | 274 |
| Words on the first screen | 153 | 156 | 150 |
| Screens to the bottom | 4.2 | 2.8 | 2.8 |

Counted by a script inside the page, not by eye. Specs view: controls 20 -> 16, words 108 -> 99.

Dashboard package tests: 180 before the redesign, 204 after. None was deleted; markup pins were rewritten
against the new markup, each seen red first.

### Review rounds

- Round 1, design conformance: 0 high, 4 medium, 5 low, plus a typed close glyph. Behaviour regression:
  1 high (the drawer's Copy command button had no click handler), 3 medium (Esc on the open View menu
  also left full screen or closed the drawer; choosing an owner left the View badge stale; an alarm
  arriving after a fold was closed did not reopen it). All fixed.
- Round 2 (the last): design 2 low (segmented control 40px tall, a typed close glyph on the section
  chip); behaviour clean, its round-1 findings verified in a browser, 1 low (an alarm that cleared and
  returned left a fold closed). All fixed.

## Port fallback

`tp dashboard` without `--port` binds a free port when 7377 is taken and says so on stderr; a port named
with `--port` is never swapped. Dogfooded with 7377 held by a running dashboard: the unpinned run served
on a system-picked port, the pinned run exited 1 naming the taken port.

## Engine test races

Three tests were red on `main` at v1.26.0 on this machine, none from a driver bug:
`TestCrash_DuringAWorktreeSetup` (the driver died before a spawned unit claimed its record slot, so the
wait saw zero of zero; red 5 of 5 alone), record-order comparisons in a refill crash test and the
`run_max_units` rolling test (records arrive in claim order, not spawn order), and a batch test that
assumed which unit exits first. After the fixes the pattern held 170 of 170 at `-count=10`.
