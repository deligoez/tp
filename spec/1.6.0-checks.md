# 1.6.0 — acceptance checks, in shape

> Written by the field project in generic terms (no names from its graph) and turned into synthetic
> test fixtures, one test per check. Notation: an edge "a→b" means a depends on b.

## Slice 0 (F4, B2, B3)

- C0.1 A condition with no dependencies outside a track's first milestone: `no-dependencies` is
  silent; a work epic in the same place still warns.
- C0.2 A milestone with one condition and three work epics, the condition and two work epics done:
  `share_done` 0.67, `throughput` counts 2, `conditions_done` 1.
- C0.3 A bound file with one closed task (estimate 30) and one wip task (estimate 20):
  `estimate_total` 50, `estimate_minutes` 30.

## Slice 1 (F1)

- C1.1 Track [M1, M2]; four roots in M1, graph order r4, r3, r2, r1: r1 heads a chain of five
  not-done epics across M1 and M2; r2 has ten direct leaf dependents and a chain of two; r3 has the
  same chain of two and three dependents; r4 has none. `tp epic ready` orders r1, r2, r3, r4, with
  r1's `longest_chain` 5 (the root excluded). Made equal in chain and dependents, r2 and r3 fall
  back to graph order.
- C1.2 With a done record on the second epic of r1's chain: r1's `longest_chain` is 1, and
  `dependents` leaves out the done epic and whatever is reachable only through it; a descendant
  also reachable through another undone path still counts.
- C1.3 With M2 closed: `dependents_open` counts only M1 epics; `dependents` counts both.
- C1.4 A deferrable dependent counts in `dependents`; a condition nothing depends on adds nothing.
- C1.5 `tp epic status`'s top-level `critical_path` is r1's chain: the longest over roots of
  `longest_chain + 1` epics, crossing milestones and ending in M2.

## Slice 2 (F2)

- C2.1 Track [P, Q, R], `opens_after {R: [P]}`: with P green and Q red, P, Q and R are open; with
  P red, only P is.
- C2.2 One track [L1, L2, L3], each `[]`, every gate red and nothing done: all three are open.
- C2.3 Tracks [X, Y] and [Z], `opens_after {Z: [Y]}`: Z opens only when Y is open (X green) and
  Y is green; X green with Y red leaves Z closed.
- C2.4 An unknown name, a two-cycle `{P: [Q], Q: [P]}` and a self-reference `{P: [P]}` are one lint
  error each, and the writers refuse them.
- C2.5 C2.1 plus two R epics with direct edges to Q epics: exactly one `partial-parallel` finding
  naming (R, Q) and listing both R epics, severity `info` because R's entry is explicit (a warning
  when the parallelism comes from defaults); with no R→Q edge, none. Only direct edges count.
- C2.6 `tp epic status`: P reports `opens_after []`, Q `[P]` (the default), R `[P]` (explicit);
  `waiting_on` lists the named milestones not yet green, and is `[]` once open.
- C2.7 `later-milestone-dependency` still follows track order; an R→Q edge is not a lint error.
- C2.8 A bound file in an open R whose epic depends on an undone Q epic: `tp next` reports
  `blocked_by_epics`.

## Slice 3 (F3)

- C3.1 Condition c; work epic w depends on c and on undone work epic u; c's milestone is open:
  `operator.conditions` is empty and `--all` lists c with `actionable: false`.
- C3.2 Once u is done, c is listed with `actionable: true`.
- C3.3 c's own dependency undone: not actionable.
- C3.4 c's milestone closed: not actionable.
- C3.5 c with no dependents and no dependencies, its milestone open: actionable.
- C3.6 w depends on conditions c1 and c2 and on nothing else undone: both are listed.
- C3.7 A done condition is listed in neither mode.
