# tp 1.15.1 — balanced KPI rows and one-line milestone cards: measurements

A fix-track patch to `tp dashboard`'s Epics tab. Two things the operator asked for after 1.15.0: the
KPI row wrapping with a lone card, and milestone cards still tall after 1.14.1's three-line clamp.
This file records, as conclusions, how each was reproduced, what changed and what shows it. Every
figure is from the synthetic graph; no field number is used.

## The synthetic graph

1.14.1's generator unchanged: 309 epics (8 of them conditions) in 14 milestones and 4 tracks, every
gate with a purpose, and long threshold and purpose texts on A2, B2 and C2. It and its setup script
live outside the repository with the agent that ran the cycle. Screenshots were taken at 1440x900,
1920x1080 and 390x844, and at 1300, 1200, 1000 and 640 px for the KPI row, light theme, before (the
1.15.0 binary) and after (this branch).

## Findings

Each new test was seen red before its fix: the KPI tests on the missing `balancedColumns` and
`lastSpans` and the absent CSS, the milestone tests on the missing `msLine`, the old `clamp`,
`measureCards` and `data-msx` strings, and the drawer without a `dkind` header.

The tests of 1.14.1's clamp went with it (`TestPage_AMilestoneCardClampsLongTextAndExpands`,
`TestPure_ACardKnowsItIsCutOnlyOnceLaidOut`, `TestPage_TheCardControlIsMeasuredAgainOnceLaidOut`,
`TestPage_ACardControlNamesItsMilestone`, `TestPage_AGatesThresholdAndPurposeReadInFull`), and the
toggle-key and focus tests name the card's `data-ms`.

## Keyboard and focus

No milestone detail existed, so the card reuses the epic drawer rather than a second panel. Checked
in the browser at 1440 and 390: Enter on A2's card opens the drawer headed "Milestone", focus on its
close button and the card's `aria-expanded` true; Escape closes it and focus returns to A2's card,
`aria-expanded` false. An epic picked from a list while a milestone is shown takes the drawer over.
The card's line is cut at every measured width, and no width scrolls the page sideways.

## Gate

`./scripts/check-suite-state.sh` (under `GOFLAGS=-p=2`), `golangci-lint run`,
`./scripts/check-deadcode.sh` and `./scripts/check-complexity.sh` each exit 0 on the last commit, no
baseline entry added. `go fix ./...` rewrote `EpochPosition`'s loop to `slices.Backward`; that
rewrite was reverted, since gocritic's rangeValCopy flags it. `.claude-plugin/plugin.json` is 1.15.1,
as every release bumps it. No documented command, flag or field changed.
