# tp 1.15.1 — balanced KPI rows and one-line milestone cards: measurements

A fix-track patch to `tp dashboard`'s Epics tab. Two things the operator asked for after 1.15.0: the
KPI row wrapping with a lone card, and milestone cards still tall after 1.14.1's three-line clamp.
This file records, as conclusions, how each was reproduced, what changed and what shows it. Every
figure is from the synthetic graph; no field number is used.

## The synthetic graph

1.14.1's generator unchanged: 309 epics (8 of them conditions) in 14 milestones and 4 tracks, every
gate with a purpose, and long threshold and purpose texts on A2, B2 and C2. It and its setup script
live outside the repository with the agent that ran the cycle. Screenshots were taken at 1440x900,
1920x1080 and 390x844, and at 1300, 1200, 1000 and 640 px for the KPI row, light theme, before (the
1.15.0 binary) and after (this branch).

The first pass of finding 1 balanced the row count but left the rows ragged: 5 + 4 with an empty
fifth cell, and the milestone cards untouched. The operator's wording was that a second row is
filled too; `cardSpans` replaced the first pass's `lastSpans`, which only stretched a lone last card.

Each new test was seen red before its fix: on a missing pure function (`balancedColumns`,
`lastSpans`, `cardSpans`, `msLine`, `milestoneHTML`, `thresholdLine`, `drawerNext`), on the old
`clamp`, `measureCards` and `data-msx` strings, or on the page's own text missing its new form.

The tests of 1.14.1's clamp went with it (`TestPage_AMilestoneCardClampsLongTextAndExpands`,
`TestPure_ACardKnowsItIsCutOnlyOnceLaidOut`, `TestPage_TheCardControlIsMeasuredAgainOnceLaidOut`,
`TestPage_ACardControlNamesItsMilestone`, `TestPage_AGatesThresholdAndPurposeReadInFull`), and the
toggle-key and focus tests name the card's `data-ms`.

## Keyboard, focus and the address

No milestone detail existed, so the card reuses the epic drawer rather than a second panel. The
drawer's state, the milestone shown and the epic selected, moves only by `tpPure.drawerNext`
(`TestPure_TheDrawerMovesBetweenEpicAndMilestone`): a card opens or closes its milestone, an epic
takes over, and a milestone a push no longer carries is dropped, so a later push restoring it
cannot reopen the drawer and take focus. The milestone shown is the address's `ms`, a place key.

Checked in the browser at 1440 and 390: Enter on A2's card opens the drawer headed "Milestone",
its info and close buttons naming a milestone, focus on the close button and the card's
`aria-expanded` true; Escape closes it and focus returns to A2's card, `aria-expanded` false. An epic
picked from a list while a milestone is shown takes the drawer over. Opening A2 writes `ms=A2`; a
reload keeps the drawer on A2, back closes it and forward reopens it. The milestone dropped by a
push is shown by the reducer's test only; the synthetic graph was not edited to drop one live. The
card's line is cut at every measured width, and no width scrolls the page sideways.

## Review

One review of the diff found nothing critical or high. Its medium and low findings were fixed, each
with a test seen red first: the milestone detail moved into pure.js with an escaping test, the
drawer's moves into a pure reducer, a vanished milestone no longer reopens the drawer, the milestone
shown lives in the address, a grid fit writes only what changed beside a stable scrollbar gutter,
the drawer's info button names a milestone, and the card and the drawer share one threshold source.
