# v1.27.1 measurements

A dashboard patch from an operator's hand test on a real plan of several hundred epics. Every number
below was measured on a synthetic fixture built to the same shape (406 epics with long multi-word
Turkish ids and titles, four choose-one groups, three review slices stale at their cap, one spec
stale at its review cap with an escalation), never on the requesting project.

## Steps wires

The Spec view's Steps mode drew each wire as a straight line over the cards, so a wire to a later
row crossed every card between. Wires now route through the gutters (`tpPure.routeWire`): down from
the source into the band under its row, across, and down a free gutter into the band over the
target's row, with rounded corners and a 4px offset per wire sharing a band or gutter. The SVG sits
under the cards. Browser probe on the fixture: 0 crossings of a card or step header at 1440 and
390, light and dark (3 or more before); the naive straight-line route gives 24 in the pure layout
test. At 390 the wires bundle in the 16px left margin: no crossings, but crowded.

## Epic map

The map's edges took the same router for a while, which cleared 6 of the fixture's 16 edges running
under a node they do not join, but on a real plan of several hundred epics and thousands of edges it
drew a grid of long square lines. The map went back to v1.27.0's curves (still deck.gl, still under
the nodes); only the Steps panel routes its wires. A task-dependency graph in deck.gl is a separate
decision, not taken here.

## Decisions

- **Large drawer, not a modal.** The drawer widens in place (Widen / Narrow in its header, kept per
  viewer in localStorage) so the plan stays beside it and Escape and focus work as before. Wide, it
  takes `min(1200px, 100vw - 64px)` with the epic's text in a 40rem column in the body type (about
  60 to 65 characters a line) and its links beside it; below 900px there is no Widen button, the
  drawer already being the viewport's width.
- **Every now section folds.** Ready epics and Operator decisions fold like the other sections,
  open by default and remembered per viewer; Operator decisions opens as an alarm while it holds a
  decision. The ready and most-waited-on lists show five rows and fold the rest.
- **Engine reason text never shows in plain view.** A blocker code reads as plain words in each
  language; the engine's sentence sits in a tooltip and its commands behind a copy icon at the end
  of the row's line, which says Copied after a press. Engine next-step prose that carries no command
  still shows as the engine writes it, in English in both languages.
- **A blocker pauses only the step it stops.** From `internal/engine/resumeblockers.go`:
  no-ready-task and epic-blocked stop Implement; review-carried-blocking and review-budget-exhausted
  stand only in review, audit-budget-exhausted only in audit. spec-stale stands in implement and
  audit but refuses only a new review round, so it rings the Review step gold as a warning while
  Implement keeps its progress; at the review cap it says implementation can continue and raising the
  cap is the operator's. An unknown code still pauses the step it stands in.

## Choose-one groups

Options run inline, "or" between them in a quiet word, a title dropped when it only repeats the id
and otherwise cut at 18ch with the whole in its tooltip. On the fixture at 1440 a group of three
takes two lines (56px, three before) and a group of two one; at 390 each option still takes its own
lines, the ids alone being wider than the column.
