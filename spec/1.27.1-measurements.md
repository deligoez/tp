# v1.27.1 measurements

A dashboard patch from an operator's hand test on a real plan of several hundred epics. Every number
below was measured on a synthetic fixture built to the same shape (406 epics with long multi-word
Turkish ids and titles, four choose-one groups, three review slices stale at their cap, one spec
stale at its review cap with an escalation), never on the requesting project.

## Steps wires

The Spec view's Steps mode drew each wire as a straight line over the cards, so a wire to a later
row crossed every card between. Wires now route through the gutters (`tpPure.routeWire`): down from
the source into the band under its row, across, and down a free gutter into the band over the
target's row, with rounded corners and a 4px offset per wire sharing a band or gutter. The SVG sits
under the cards. Browser probe on the fixture: 0 crossings of a card or step header at 1440 and
390, light and dark (3 or more before); the naive straight-line route gives 24 in the pure layout
test. At 390 the wires bundle in the 16px left margin: no crossings, but crowded.

## Epic map

deck.gl drew edges under opaque nodes, but 6 of the fixture map's 16 edges ran under a node they do
not join. Edges now take the same router (`tpPure.wirePoints`); back edges keep their curve. After:
0 of 16, over 3570 sampled points.

## Decisions

- **Large drawer, not a modal.** The drawer widens in place (Widen / Narrow in its header, kept per
  viewer in localStorage) so the plan stays beside it and Escape and focus work as before. Wide, it
  takes `min(1200px, 100vw - 64px)` with the epic's text in a 40rem column in the body type (about
  60 to 65 characters a line) and its links beside it; below 900px there is no Widen button, the
  drawer already being the viewport's width.
- **Operator decisions are not folded.** Each is something only the operator can do, so the panel
  shows all of them; the ready and most-waited-on lists fold after five rows.
- **Engine reason text never shows in plain view.** A blocker code reads as plain words in each
  language; the engine's sentence sits in a tooltip and its commands behind Copy command. Engine
  next-step prose that carries no command still shows as the engine writes it, in English in both
  languages.
