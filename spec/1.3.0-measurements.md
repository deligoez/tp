## Pre-registered stop rules

Written before the first round, so a later decision to stop is read against them.

- The ground loop runs before the first counted review round and ends when `--status --check` exits
  0 and the latest round's breakdown holds no unrepaired `FAIL` or `PARTIAL`.
- Review converges at two clean rounds under `review_converge_on: blocking`, or stops at the cap of
  three. At the cap, open findings about behaviour this release creates become task acceptance and
  decomposition proceeds; a critical or high finding about a *decision* stops the cycle for the
  operator.
- When the same section fails three rounds, the problem is the design, not the text: the section is
  cut or the decision changed, never reworded a fourth time.
- The spec and this file do not grow after round 1 (`spec-growth` is registered); a repair deletes
  or narrows.

The decisions taken at the review cap are in `spec/1.3.0-corrections.md` (D1–D13).
